We collect only what we need to run your account and deliver your orders, and we never sell your information.

What we collect

  • Account details: username, email address and a securely scrambled (hashed) password - we can't see your password.
  • Order details: the receiver's name, phone number, delivery address, the items you bought and how you paid.
  • Technical data: your IP address and actions such as logging in, kept for security (for example to block password guessing).

How we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your orders, and to contact you about them.
  • To keep your account and our shop secure.
  • M-Pesa payments are handled by Safaricom; we receive the payment confirmation, never your M-Pesa PIN.

We share delivery details only with the people who deliver your order. We don't sell or rent your data.

How long we keep it

We keep order records as long as the law requires for tax and accounting. Security logs are kept for a limited time. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 you can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and object to how we use it. Contact us {% if SHOP.email %}at {{ SHOP.email }}{% else %}using the details on our contact page{% endif %}.

Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you logged in, remember your cart and protect forms. We don't use advertising cookies.
